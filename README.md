# Hamelberg AI

The public home for Kedma Hamelberg’s applied AI research, evidence-led analysis and public-facing projects.

## Contribution

This site connects the AI Empowerment Observatory, The Brief and European Security Monitor: projects that translate complex AI developments into inspectable evidence, careful interpretation and usable public knowledge.

**AI as methodology:** the portfolio develops auditable workflows that combine computational analysis, human judgment and transparent source practice.

**AI as a research phenomenon:** it investigates how AI affects capability, autonomy, communication, decision-making and social power.

**Teaching and impact:** the site offers accessible entry points for students, researchers, decision-makers and wider publics to evaluate AI claims without losing sight of uncertainty or human consequences.
