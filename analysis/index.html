Use the notes with the live projects

The Observatory provides retained reporting and its classifications. The Brief makes source-linked stories easier to read. The European Security Monitor presents sampled security narratives. These are distinct kinds of material; their counts and labels are not interchangeable.

For a reusable way to record a claim, source, comparison and uncertainty, start with the evidence record in the reading guide. For publishing responsibility, AI assistance and how to flag a mistake, see our editorial approach.
