Privacy and advertising

What happens on this site.

This page covers hamelberg-ai.com. The linked projects have their own services and privacy notices.

Reading and links

The main site is a static set of public pages. It does not provide an account form or collect information through an on-site form. When you follow a link to the Observatory, The Brief, the European Security Monitor or Kedma Hamelberg’s personal website, your browser opens that destination and its own privacy practices apply.

The homepage links to the European Security Monitor and does not embed its dashboard. Visiting that separate project connects your browser to monitor.hamelberg-ai.com. You can read the Monitor’s privacy notice for its advertising and measurement details. The Brief also has a separate privacy notice.

Hosting

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading a page sends technical request information, including your IP address, to the hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of that information. The root site does not install analytics scripts, set advertising cookies or provide an on-site account.

Advertising

This site publishes a Google AdSense account verification tag and an ads.txt seller entry. These identify the publisher for review; they do not mean that ads are currently displayed here. The homepage does not load an AdSense ad script. If advertising is activated on this site later, this notice and any required consent controls must be updated before ad requests begin.

Contact

If you email research@hamelberg-ai.com, your email service and ours process the address and message to deliver and answer it. For a privacy question, include only the information needed to explain your request.
