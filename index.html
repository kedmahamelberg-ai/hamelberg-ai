A useful starting point

Three questions, three places to investigate.

These projects share an interest in evidence and human consequences, but they use different sources. Begin with the question you have, then follow the original material and the method behind the answer.

What does this AI news mean for people?

The Observatory groups reporting about the same development and reads evidence about people and AI separately. Its weekly picture is a bounded media sample, so a reported benefit is not proof that everyone benefits.

How the Observatory reads news ↗

What actually happened in a story?

The Brief turns the retained reporting into short, readable accounts. Open the linked source to check a claim. A company announcement, an early study and an observed result deserve different levels of confidence.

How The Brief writes ↗

How is European security discussed?

The Monitor records framing in public videos and official government communications. Its country tags describe the subject of a source, while audience comments and engagement are separate observations.
